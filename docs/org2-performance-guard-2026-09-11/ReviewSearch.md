Lifecycle: closed/empty search starts no worker or file scan. Hidden search stops work and revalidates once on return. Superseded or unmounted searches cannot commit results. An already-started shared filesystem/git request cannot be aborted here and may finish after close; the scan starts no further reads and discards its completion. Filesystem failures surface an error status. No polling, network retry, authentication, provider-ingestion, persistence or cross-machine protocol is added.

Verification: 74 tests passed across 12 targeted suites in the isolated PR worktree, including 15 review-search tests. Changed-file ESLint, test placement and dependency boundaries passed.
