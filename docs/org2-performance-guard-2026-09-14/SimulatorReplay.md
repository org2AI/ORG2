# Simulator replay ownership lifecycle

The authoritative replay state is the per-store cell replay atom. The retention writer previously evicted mounted overrides, and session removal deleted records without revoking their mounted writers. Neither issue involves persisted history; no historical cleanup or migration is needed.

Mounted replay writers now register a revocable owner for their effect lifetime. Active keys are protected from eviction; at most 256 inactive entries remain, and unmount reapplies the inactive limit. Session removal revokes matching owners before deleting their records. Their writes are rejected synchronously, and playback effects are disabled on the removal render. Registration is scoped to each Jotai store and cleanup releases the owner, including StrictMode setup/cleanup cycles.
